GOT QUESTIONS?

FREQUENTLY ASKED PRICING QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know about booking, rescheduling, and workshop seat inclusions.

What is included in the workshop ticket price?
Every ticket includes all fresh seasonal flowers, foliage, use of professional floral tools, hands-on expert instruction, a handmade ceramic vessel to take home, botanical drinks, and custom eco-friendly gift wrapping.
Can I reschedule or transfer my booking to a friend?
Yes! You can reschedule your workshop ticket or transfer it to a friend up to 48 hours before your session starts with zero penalty fees. Simply contact our studio team or manage your booking via the Guest Portal.
Do I need to bring any tools or materials with me?
Not at all! We provide everything you need at your worktable station, including organic cotton aprons, Japanese carbon-steel pruning shears, Kenzan pin frogs, and fresh seasonal blooms.
Are there group discounts for private parties or corporate events?
Yes, we offer custom tiered pricing and full studio takeover rates for private bookings of 6 or more guests. Enquire via our Private Events page to receive a custom proposal.
How long will my floral centerpiece arrangement last at home?
With our studio conditioning techniques and fresh water refills, most centerpieces remain vibrant for 7 to 14 days. We provide a complimentary floral care card with every arrangement!
