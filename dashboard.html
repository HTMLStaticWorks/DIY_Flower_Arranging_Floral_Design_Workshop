FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

How do I reschedule my workshop session?
Navigate to the "My Bookings" tab in your Guest Portal. Click "Change Date" next to your confirmed session. Free rescheduling is available up to 48 hours prior to your scheduled class.
Do I need to bring any tools or aprons?
Not at all! We provide organic cotton aprons, Japanese carbon-steel shears, ceramic vessels, and fresh seasonal blooms at your reserved worktable.
How do my Loyalty Points apply to rewards?
Every seat booked earns 100 points. Once you hit 500 points, your perk (such as a complimentary ceramic vase upgrade or free guest ticket pass) will automatically activate at checkout.
Can I transfer my booking ticket to a friend?
Yes! You can transfer your seat to a friend by sending their name and email to support@bloomandform.com or updating guest details under "My Bookings".
What is your studio cancellation policy?
Cancellations made 48 hours in advance receive full studio credit valid for 12 months. Inside 48 hours, stems are freshly cut for your arrival, but you may send a friend in your place!
